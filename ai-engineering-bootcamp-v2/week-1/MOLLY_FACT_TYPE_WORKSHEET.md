## Why this exists

The drafting tool stores case information as typed facts so it can notice when sources disagree, when something is missing, and what to put in Background & History.

Nobody who writes these reports has reviewed this list yet. Some entries were added to fix software tests, not because you use them clinically. **Your job is to correct the list** — keep, drop, merge, split, or reclassify.

**44 fact types** below. Aim for one focused hour; unfinished rows can be "parked."

---

## How to mark each row

1. **Keep?** Yes / No / Merge with ___ / Split into ___
2. **One right answer, or can people legitimately differ?**
   - **One right answer** → if two sources disagree, something is wrong (*conflict* you resolve). Example: date of birth.
   - **People can differ** → parent vs teacher vs student can disagree and that *is* the finding (*variance*), not an error. Example: inattention ratings on BASC/SRS.
3. **Does it change over time?**
   - **Lifelong / historical** — birth term, walked at 13 months; later sources should still agree.
   - **As of a date** — grade, attendance, current IEP; 2024 vs 2026 can differ without a conflict.
4. **Your notes** — better wording, missing pieces, which sources should/shouldn't assert this.

Blank columns on the right are for your marks.
